Galactic Battler

Galactic Battler is a space auto-battler: you build the fleet, the battle plays itself. Between rounds you draft ships and relics; in each round your fleet fights a fleet another commander built.

How to play

  • Draft ships and relics between rounds.
  • Build synergies so your fleet grows stronger.
  • Watch your fleet fight rival commanders round after round.

PREVIOUS UPDATE: BOSS GAUNTLET (OCT 29)

A new mode: fight through three sectors, pick your route before every battle, and take down a boss at the end of each one. Plus a look at every pilot's build on the daily leaderboard.

HOW THE GAUNTLET WORKS

  • Press GAUNTLET on the main menu and pick a commander. There are three sectors: four battles, then a boss. Beat the third boss to win.
  • Before each battle you choose between two routes: Raid (a normal fight), Elite (a much tougher fleet, win for a reward pick and +5 scrap), Salvage (an easier fleet, win for +6 scrap but no reward pick) or Repair Dock (an easier fleet, win to repair 1 hull, only when you're damaged).
  • Losing to a boss costs 2 hull. You still get a reward, then face the same boss again, a little stronger.
  • Beat a boss to pick a boss relic. Only bosses drop these.
  • Bosses can't be jammed. Gauntlet fleets don't go into the normal ghost pools.

THE BOSSES

Each run meets three of the four, in a random order. They get stronger in later sectors.

Hive Mothership
SWARM

Hits your front 2 ships and launches a fighter with every attack. Burst it down before the swarm buries you.

The Citadel
FORTRESS

Hits your whole fleet. While either Shield Pylon stands, it takes only 1 damage per hit. Ships that reach past the front line help.

Admiral's Dreadnought
ENRAGE

Broadsides hit 3 ships, behind two escorts. At half HP it enrages: twice as fast, and it hits everything.

Void Leviathan
CURSE

Hits 3 ships and curses your fleet at the start, so nothing heals. At half HP it splits off two Echoes.

EARLIER TODAY: ONLINE PLAY

Daily challenge, friend duels, ghost results, the battle report and sharper ships. Details below.

HIGHLIGHTS

DAILY CHALLENGE

  • Press DAILY on the main menu. Everyone plays the same run that day: the same commander (locked ones too, so it's a chance to try them), the same starting shop and the same enemy fleets.
  • Only your first finished run counts. The leaderboard ranks by wins, then hull left, then fewest battles.
  • A new challenge starts at midnight UTC. A daily started before midnight can still be finished and scored the next day.
  • The daily has its own save, so it never replaces your normal run.

FRIEND DUELS

  • At the end of a run, SHARE FLEET copies a link to your fleet (on phones it opens the share menu).
  • Opening the link takes your friend straight to a duel: their latest fleet against yours. The DUEL button on the menu also takes a pasted link or code.
  • Duels are just for fun: no hull, XP or ghost upload.

YOUR GHOSTS

  • When another pilot fights one of your uploaded fleets, the result is saved. The main menu tells you how your ghosts did since you last looked, and who they fought.
  • Finished runs are now recorded (commander, final fleet, relics and results, no personal details) so balance can be tuned on real games, not just simulations.

BATTLE REPORT

  • After every battle, the result bar gives one line on why it went that way, such as "Their Wraith did 77% of their damage."
  • REPORT shows both sides' damage, healing, hits blocked by shields, dodges, ships lost, top 3 damage dealers, and what took out your first ship.
  • SKIP plays out the rest of a battle at once.

SHARPER SHIPS

  • Every ship is drawn at twice the resolution, at the same size on screen, with a thinner outline and extra shading.
  • Ten ships are redrawn by hand with new detail: Scout, Gunboat, Phantom, Dreadnought, Flagship, Leviathan, Voidmother, Ion Cruiser, Eclipse and Siege Platform.

BALANCE

EASIER OPENING ROUNDS

Bot opponents shop almost perfectly from round 1, which made the start of a run feel harsh. Half of round 1 bots now open with one ship fewer, and every bot gets 1 less scrap in rounds 2 and 3. To keep runs about as hard overall, bots get their late-game bonus scrap a little sooner (+1 from round 8, +2 from 11, +3 from 14).
